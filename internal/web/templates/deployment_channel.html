About Deployment Channels

A deployment channel defines:

  • Artifact Kind - what your project produces (container, k8s manifests, etc.)
  • Hosting Platform - where it runs (Fly.io, GKE, etc.)

Mendel validates each channel before allowing demos or production deploys.
