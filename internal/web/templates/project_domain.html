Why a domain at all

Some platforms hand a deployment a name of their own — Fly.io answers with <app>.fly.dev. A Kubernetes cluster hands out an address and nothing else, and an address is not something a sign-in provider will accept: a redirect URI has to name a host, and has to be https.
